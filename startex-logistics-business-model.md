## First: What You Should Eliminate Immediately

These are **NOT** startup-friendly without experience:

### ❌ Project Cargo / Super Loads
- Too relationship-heavy
- Too operationally complex

### ❌ Bulk Hazmat / Tanker
- Higher compliance exposure
- Harder carrier onboarding

### ❌ Pharma Validated Cold Chain
- Claims risk + shipper expectations are extremely high

**Note:** These are Phase 2+ plays.

## The Honest Answer

If you are brand new with limited experience:

### **The Best Starting Lane: Flatbed – Energy Pipe & Steel**

**Why?**

- Large carrier pool in TX/LA/OK
- No hazmat authority
- No special insurance requirements
- Strong Gulf Coast density
- Energy-adjacent
- Reasonable ticket size
- Easy to scale volume

**Realistic Math:**
- 300 loads at $6,500 average = **$1.95M freight moved**
- That's extremely doable with 5–10 solid shippers
